Jakub first discovered yoga under the tropical skies of Thailand, and what started as curiosity quickly became a lifeline through depression, grief, and addiction. On the mat, he found strength, stillness, and a spark of joy.

Now certified RYT200 in yoga and RYT60 in yin yoga he is teaching from the heart (with plenty of laughs along the way), Jakub brings warmth, motivation, and genuine compassion to every class. He offers energising, feel-good flow sessions and yin-inspired melt-down practices designed to help you reset and recharge. Empowerment and feeling great is what Jakub wants you to feel…

Expect good vibes, grounding breathwork, the occasional wobbly balance, and a welcoming space where you can pause, breathe, reconnect — and maybe even surprise yourself.

- RYT60 Yin Yoga Teacher
