Amanda is a barre and yoga teacher specialising in multiple disciplines such as hot yoga, vinyasa and yin (300hr RYT), mat pilates and APPI barrecontrol. Born in the Philippines and raised by the sea in the North of England, she moved to Birmingham for her BSc in Physiotherapy and has called the city home ever since. Growing up dancing hip hop and commercial, with a musical background, movement has always been her outlet, and it's where she feels most in her element.

At Forge, Amanda teaches barre and yoga to a wide range of people, from fit adults to children and older populations, so her classes are always adaptable and welcoming. Naturally curious and drawn to anything innovative, she teaches to remind you why movement keeps you alive, to bring you joy and confidence, and to help you find your spark again. Every class is a homecoming to your body and mindset.

Her style is spicy, intelligent, creative, motivational and nurturing. Her tip for newcomers: come with an open mind, show up without a performance, and expect a big smile.
