Hadiya first discovered Pilates at a classical Pilates studio and quickly fell in love with the method. Working a desk-based job, she found it eased the stiffness of sitting all day and helped her feel more connected to her body. That experience inspired her to qualify as a mat instructor and share her love of Pilates with others.

She loves how mat Pilates combines mindful movement with a real challenge, encouraging you to slow down, move with intention and discover what your body is capable of. Hadiya's classes are welcoming and supportive, with a focus on helping women feel comfortable on the mat, whatever their experience or starting point.

## Experience
- Level 3 Mat Pilates qualification
