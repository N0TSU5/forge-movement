Lamia started doing Pilates without knowing it was Pilates.

It was Covid, she was 15, and she typed "at home workout no equipment" as everybody did into YouTube, she was like a woman on a mission (a very bored, very stuck-inside mission). Somewhere between her bedroom  room floor and away from her siblings eyes, something clicked. It was the one thing she could actually control, and she fell head over heels for it. It was also the first time she fell in love with her body, which is a big deal at 15.

Then Covid ended, life got busy, and a couple of years zoomed by. Eventually she discovered that her beloved no-equipment routine had a name: Pilates. It was never just a workout. It's a system, a method, a flow, an intention, and a whole mentality. Basically, it's a lifestyle with better posture.

Then came the reformer, and she was gone. The more she did it, the more she wanted everyone else to feel what she felt: more aware, more in control, and a little more in love with how their body moves.

Now she's APPI-trained and ready to share the magic. Control and intention is her drive. (Also, snacks after class. But mostly control and intention.)
