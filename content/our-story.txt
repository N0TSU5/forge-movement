Forge Movement was created from a simple belief: your body is worth investing in at every stage of life.

We bring together Pilates, yoga, barre, strength and infrared heat to create a space where movement is purposeful, intelligent and accessible.

Whether you are beginning your movement journey, looking to build strength, improve mobility or simply feel better in your body, Forge is a place to learn, move and grow, without judgement and without the pressure to be perfect.

## Why *Forge*?
Because movement should be more than something you simply do.

It should help you understand your body, build confidence in what it can do and give you the tools to keep moving well for life.

At Forge, we believe strength and mobility belong together. So do movement and recovery.

Our classes are designed to complement one another, combining the precision and control of Pilates, the mobility and mindfulness of yoga, the strength and rhythm of barre, and the restorative benefits of infrared heat.

**Different disciplines. One stronger you.**
