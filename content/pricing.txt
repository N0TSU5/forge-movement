- The 20% discount applies only to additional classes purchased directly by active members after their monthly credits are used.

- Unused membership credits and add-on purchases follow separate, clearly stated expiry rules.
- 25% discount for full-time students, carers and employees of registered charity organisations. Please bring ID or a DWP letter on your first visit.
