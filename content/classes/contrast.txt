A unique Pilates experience combining Reformer and mat work within the same session, allowing you to explore how the body moves, feels and responds to each.

Exercises are explored both with and without the support and resistance of the Reformer. You'll discover how to create and maintain the same sense of connection, tension and control on the mat, and how the Reformer can enhance that experience through its springs and support.

Props and modifications are thoughtfully incorporated to create continuity between the two methods, while highlighting the contrast between them. The result is a deeper understanding of your movement, strength and control, both with and without the apparatus.

Suitable for all levels.

## What to bring
- Grip socks (required, for hygiene reasons)
- Non-slip gloves (required, for hygiene reasons)
- Comfortable, fitted clothing
- A water bottle
