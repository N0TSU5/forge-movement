A mat-based Pilates class focused on building and maintaining a strong connection between mind and body. Each movement is performed with intention and control, encouraging greater awareness of how and why you move.

Breath is woven into every exercise, with particular emphasis on controlled exhalation and the power of the core, or "powerhouse", to support and enhance movement. As you progress, you'll develop a deeper understanding of how breath, core connection and mindful movement can help you access your body's full potential, within your own range of movement.

Suitable for all abilities, with modifications and props provided throughout.

## What to bring
- Comfortable, fitted clothing you can move in
- Grip socks (recommended)
- A water bottle
- A small towel
