category: Why Pilates
q: What is Pilates?
---
Pilates is a method of exercise built on control, precision and breath. It strengthens the deep core muscles (the "powerhouse") that support every movement, while improving posture, mobility and body awareness.
===
category: Why Pilates
q: Is Pilates suitable for everyone?
---
Yes. All of our classes are suitable for all abilities, with modifications and props provided throughout. Your instructor will help you work within your own range of movement.
===
category: Why Pilates
q: Can I do Pilates if I have an injury, a medical condition or I'm pregnant?
---
Often, yes, but please check with your doctor or physiotherapist first, and always tell your instructor before class so they can offer suitable modifications.
===
category: Classes
q: Which class should I start with?
---
If you're new to Pilates, Forge Pilates (mat) is a great foundation. Forge Reformer is suitable for all abilities too. If you're after recovery and mobility, try Forge Restore or Forge Reset. See our First Timer page for a full guide.
===
category: Classes
q: How big are the classes?
---
Reformer classes are kept to a maximum of five people, so your instructor can give you real attention. Capacity for mat and barre classes is shown on the booking site, as each class uses a different studio layout. Please check the Momence app.
===
category: Classes
q: Do I need prior experience?
---
Not at all. Every class is suitable for beginners, and exercises are layered progressively so you can build at your own pace.
===
category: Classes
q: What is the infrared studio?
---
Our infrared heated studio uses ceiling-mounted panels to emit invisible light waves that warm your body directly instead of blowing hot air into the room. This creates a deeply therapeutic environment specifically optimized for flexibility, muscle recovery, and mindful movement during Pilates and yoga practices. Bring water and light clothing.
===

category: Prepare for your class
q: What should I wear?
---
Comfortable, fitted clothing you can move and stretch in. Loose tops can catch on Reformer springs.
===
category: Prepare for your class
q: Do I need grip socks?
---
Grip socks are required for Forge Reformer and Forge Contrast for hygiene reasons, and non-slip gloves are also required for Forge Contrast. Grip socks and gloves are available to purchase at our studio. Just ask your instructor.
===
category: Prepare for your class
q: How early should I arrive?
---
Please arrive 10–15 minutes before your first class so you can settle in and talk to your instructor about any injuries or concerns. For later classes, please arrive on time: the door is locked once class starts, and the instructor won't be able to open it for latecomers.
===
category: Prepare for your class
q: Are there showers or changing facilities?
---
There are female and male changing rooms on the gym floor. New clients will be given a short tour of the facility on their first day with us.
