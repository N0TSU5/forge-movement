heading: The Forge *approach*
---
Forge Movement isn't about one type of exercise. It's about building a body that moves well, feels strong and knows how to recover.
===
id: move
title: Move
tagline: Move with intention.
summary: We focus on quality of movement rather than simply moving for the sake of moving.
---
Pilates, yoga and barre encourage greater awareness, control, mobility and connection. We focus on quality of movement rather than simply moving for the sake of moving.

Because understanding how you move can change the way you move through everything else.
===
id: strength
title: Strength
tagline: Build a body that can do more.
summary: Strength isn't just about lifting heavier. It's about developing a body that feels capable, resilient and confident.
---
Strength isn't just about lifting heavier. It's about developing a body that feels capable, resilient and confident.

At Forge, we believe strength should support everyday life, helping you move with greater freedom, stability and confidence.
===
id: recover
title: Recover
tagline: Recovery isn't an afterthought. It's part of the work.
summary: Our infrared-heated studio is designed to complement your movement practice and encourage relaxation and recovery.
---
Our infrared-heated studio creates a warm environment designed to complement your movement practice and encourage relaxation and recovery.

Because becoming stronger isn't only about what you ask your body to do. It's also about how well you allow it to recover.
